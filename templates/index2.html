Design your own mushroom and find it!

As 🍂fall🍂 progresses, 🍄mushrooms🍄 begin to sprout from the ground, and people head out of their homes to go mushroom hunting🥾🍃.

For some, it’s a simple family activity; for others, a passion for nature. But caution, distinguishing edible species from poisonous ones requires meticulous attention to detail; there is no single characteristic by which all toxic mushrooms can be identified, nor one by which all edible mushrooms can be identified.

Whether you're looking for them in grasslands, leaves, meadows, along paths, in urban areas, in woods, or even in waste areas, this catalog will help you estimate the likelihood of finding the rare mushroom you have in mind.

Mushroom catalogue

The data set includes descriptions of hypothetical samples corresponding to 23 species of gilled mushrooms in the Agaricus and Lepiota Family. Each species is identified as definitely edible, definitely poisonous, or of unknown edibility and not recommended. This latter class was combined with the poisonous one. The data were taken from The Audubon Society Field Guide to North American Mushrooms
